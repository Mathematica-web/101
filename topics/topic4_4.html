Algebra is just a language. Let's learn how to translate it!

Translating English to Math

Mathematical equations are just real-life situations written in a secret code. By assigning variables (like x and y) to unknown quantities, we can turn entire paragraphs into short, powerful equations.

English Word / Phrase Algebraic Translation
"Is", "Results in", "Total" Equals sign ( = )
"Twice", "Three times", "Product" Multiplication ( e.g., 2x, 3y )
"More than", "Sum", "Added to" Addition ( + )
"Less than", "Difference" Subtraction ( - )
"Cost of one item", "Distance traveled" Variables ( x or y )

🚕 1. The Taxi Fare Formulator

A taxi charges a fixed base fare just to get in the car, plus a rate for every kilometer you travel. Let the total distance be x, and the total fare be y. Adjust the rates below to build the linear equation!

"The total fare (y) is ₹10 per km (x), plus a base charge of ₹50."
Step 1: Direct Translation y = 10x + 50
Step 2: Standard Form (ax + by + c = 0) Move everything to one side: 10x - y + 50 = 0

💬 2. The Algebra Translator

Let's translate a common word problem comparing two items. Let the cost of the first item be x and the second item be y. Change the words in the sentence to instantly see the algebra change!

"The cost of a Notebook (x) is the cost of a Pen (y) ₹5."
Direct Translation: x = 2y + 5
Standard Form (ax + by + c = 0): x - 2y - 5 = 0

⏳ 3. The Age Problem Time-Machine

Age problems are classic algebra puzzles! Let the Father's current age be x and the Son's current age be y. Adjust the time machine to see how their ages shift and form a linear equation.

"5 years ago, the father's age was 3 times the son's age."
Step 1: Shift Both Ages in Time Father = (x - 5), Son = (y - 5)
Step 2: Apply the Multiplier (x - 5) = 3(y - 5)
Step 3: Expand and convert to Standard Form x - 3y + 10 = 0

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: "The sum of two numbers is 18." Write a linear equation in two variables to represent this statement.
Step 1: Identify the unknowns. Let the first number be x, and the second number be y.
Step 2: Translate the word "sum" to addition (+), and the word "is" to equals (=).
Step 3: Write the direct equation: x + y = 18.
Step 4: To put it in standard form, move 18 to the left side.
Final Answer: x + y - 18 = 0
Q2: "Fahrenheit (F) temperature is 32 more than 1.8 times the Celsius (C) temperature." Write this as an equation.
Step 1: Let the variables be F and C.
Step 2: Translate "1.8 times Celsius": 1.8C.
Step 3: Translate "32 more than": + 32.
Step 4: Combine it all based on the sentence.
Final Answer: F = 1.8C + 32 (which is the actual scientific formula for temperature conversion!)
