🥣 Hollow vs. Solid Hemisphere

A hollow hemisphere (like an empty bowl) exposes both an inner and outer curved surface. A solid hemisphere (like a paperweight) has one outer curved surface and a solid flat lid!

Outer CSA (2πr²) 402.1
Inner CSA (2πr²) 402.1

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: A perfectly hollow, thin-shelled hemispherical bowl needs to be painted inside and out. If the radius is 'r', what is the total area to be painted?
4πr²

Because the bowl is completely hollow, you must paint the outer curve (2πr²) AND the inner curve (2πr²). The total painted surface is 4πr²—the exact same surface area as a full sphere!
Q2: Why is the Total Surface Area (TSA) of a solid hemisphere 3πr²?
Because of the flat lid!

When you cut a solid sphere in half, you expose a brand new flat, circular surface. The curved dome part is half of a sphere (4πr² ÷ 2 = 2πr²). The new flat circular top has an area of πr².
Total = 2πr² (curved) + πr² (flat) = 3πr².
