The Rules of Integer Operations

Integers represent directions. Positive numbers move you forward, while negative numbers move you backward. Memorize these golden rules for working with signs:

Operation Rule Example
Addition (Same Signs) Add the numbers and keep the same sign. -3 + (-4) = -7
Addition (Different Signs) Subtract the smaller number from the larger. Keep the sign of the larger number. -8 + 5 = -3
Multiplication / Division Same signs = Positive (+)
Different signs = Negative (-)		 (-4) × (-2) = +8
(-4) × (2) = -8

🏠 3. Absolute Value (Distance from Zero)

Absolute value, written as |x|, measures pure distance from zero, regardless of direction. You can walk 15 steps backward, but you still walked 15 steps! Distance is never negative.

Absolute Value Distance: |x| |-15| = 15

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: What is the result of -15 - (-8)?
Step 1: Identify the double negative. Subtracting a negative is exactly the same as adding a positive.
Step 2: Rewrite the equation: -15 + 8.
Step 3: The signs are different, so subtract the smaller from the larger (15 - 8 = 7) and keep the sign of the larger (-).
Final Answer: -7
Q2: Evaluate: |-10| + |5| × |-2|.
Step 1: Convert all absolute values into standard positive numbers.
|-10| becomes 10. |5| remains 5. |-2| becomes 2.
Step 2: Rewrite equation: 10 + 5 × 2.
Step 3: Apply Order of Operations (BODMAS). Multiply first: 5 × 2 = 10.
Step 4: Add: 10 + 10.
Final Answer: 20
