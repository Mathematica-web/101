🏛️ Euclid's Universal Axioms

The indisputable rules that govern all of mathematics!

The 7 Axioms

While Postulates only apply to shapes and geometry, Axioms are universal truths. They apply to numbers, algebra, physics, and everyday logic. Euclid listed seven fundamental axioms that form the bedrock of all mathematical equations.

Axiom # Statement Simple Meaning
1 Things which are equal to the same thing are equal to one another. If A = C and B = C, then A = B.
2 If equals are added to equals, the wholes are equal. If A = B, then A + C = B + C.
3 If equals are subtracted from equals, the remainders are equal. If A = B, then A - C = B - C.
4 Things which coincide with one another are equal to one another. If two shapes fit perfectly over each other, they are identical.
5 The whole is greater than the part. A full pizza is always bigger than a single slice.
6 & 7 Things which are double (or halves) of the same things are equal to one another. If A = B, then 2A = 2B and A/2 = B/2.

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: Which Axiom allows us to solve the equation x + 5 = 12 by subtracting 5 from both sides?
Axiom 3!

"If equals are subtracted from equals, the remainders are equal." By subtracting 5 from the left side (x + 5) and 5 from the right side (12), we guarantee that the new equation (x = 7) remains perfectly true and balanced.
Q2: If line segment PQ is placed exactly on top of line segment RS and they match end-to-end, what does Axiom 4 tell us?
They are mathematically equal!

Axiom 4 ("Things which coincide with one another are equal") proves that length PQ = length RS without even needing a ruler to measure them!
