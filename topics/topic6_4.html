📐 Angle Sum Property of a Triangle

The magical rule that holds every triangle in the universe together!

The Triangle Rules

A triangle is one of the most stable and fundamental shapes in geometry. No matter how you stretch, squish, or distort a flat triangle, the relationship between its angles is locked by two unbreakable theorems.

Theorem What it Means
Angle Sum Property The sum of the three interior angles of a triangle is always exactly 180°. (Theorem 6.7)
Exterior Angle Theorem If a side of a triangle is extended, the exterior angle formed is exactly equal to the sum of the two interior opposite angles. (Theorem 6.8)

🔺 1. The 180° Rule

Move the top vertex of the triangle around. Watch how the three interior angles constantly shift size, yet their total sum remains perfectly anchored at 180°!

Interior Angle Sum Property Angle A + Angle B + Angle C = 180°
Perimeter = 0 units Notice how the outer perimeter changes, but the interior angle sum never breaks!

🚀 2. The Exterior Angle Theorem

Extend the base of a triangle outward to create an Exterior Angle. Watch how this outside angle is always a perfect combination of the two interior opposite angles!

Exterior Angle Theorem Exterior = Angle A + Angle B

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: Can a triangle have two right angles (90°)?
Step 1: According to the Angle Sum Property, the total of all three interior angles must be exactly 180°.
Step 2: If a triangle had two right angles, those two angles alone would equal 180° (90° + 90° = 180°).
Step 3: This leaves 0° for the third angle, meaning the shape would collapse into a flat line!
Final Answer: No! A triangle can only ever have a maximum of one right angle or one obtuse angle.
Q2: In a triangle, if interior angles A and B are 40° and 60°, what is the exterior angle at vertex C?
100°!

Thanks to the Exterior Angle Theorem, we don't even need to calculate the interior angle of C. We simply add the two opposite interior angles: 40° + 60° = 100°.
