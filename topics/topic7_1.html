📐 Introduction to Congruence

When two shapes are absolutely, perfectly identical!

What Does "Congruent" Mean?

In geometry, two figures are exactly congruent (symbol: ≅) if they have the exact same shape and the exact same size. If you were to cut one out and place it over the other, they would completely cover each other without any gaps or overlaps.

Concept Real-Life Example
Congruent Lines Two fresh, unsharpened pencils from the exact same box.
Congruent Circles Two identical 10-Rupee coins.
Congruent Triangles Two identical triangular sandwiches cut from the same square bread.

🔍 2. The Golden Rule: CPCT

CPCT stands for "Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles". It means that if two triangles are proven to be identical, then all their matching sides and matching angles are automatically equal. Select a part to see its match!

✍️ 3. The Order Matters

In geometry, if we write ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR, the order of the letters is a strict rule. It means Vertex A maps exactly to Vertex P, B maps to Q, and C maps to R. 🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: If we know that ΔXYZ ≅ ΔLMN, what side is strictly equal to side YZ?
Side MN!

Because the order of letters dictates the mapping, the last two letters of the first triangle (Y and Z) perfectly correspond to the last two letters of the second triangle (M and N). By CPCT, Side YZ = Side MN.
Q2: Can two circles with different radii be congruent?
No.

Congruence requires both the exact same shape AND the exact same size. While all circles have the same shape, different radii mean they have different sizes. They would not perfectly superimpose over one another.
