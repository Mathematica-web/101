Mastering surface areas and volumes in 3D space!

The Core Formulas

Measurement Cuboid (Length l, Width w, Height h) Cube (Edge a)
Volume (V)
Space inside		 l × w × h a³
Lateral Surface Area (LSA)
4 side walls only (no top/bottom)		 2h(l + w) 4a²
Total Surface Area (TSA)
All 6 faces		 2(lw + wh + hl) 6a²

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: A room has dimensions 5m x 4m x 3m (L x W x H). What is the total area of its 4 walls?
54 m²

The area of the 4 walls is the Lateral Surface Area (LSA).
LSA = 2h(l + w)
LSA = 2 × 3(5 + 4) = 6 × 9 = 54 m².
Q2: If the edge of a cube is doubled, how many times does its volume increase?
8 times!

Original Volume = a³
New Volume = (2a)³ = 2³ × a³ = 8a³.
