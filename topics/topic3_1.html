🗺️ Introduction to Coordinate Geometry

Turning geometry into algebra using the magical Cartesian Plane!

The Map of Mathematics

To precisely locate any point on a flat surface, we use a grid system called the Cartesian Plane. It's like a city map with horizontal streets and vertical avenues!

Component Definition
X-Axis & Y-Axis The horizontal line is the X-axis. The vertical line is the Y-axis. Together, they divide the plane into four sections.
The Origin The exact center where the two axes intersect. Its coordinates are always (0, 0).
Coordinates (x, y) An ordered pair that acts as a GPS address. The first number (x-coordinate or abscissa) tells you how far left/right to go. The second number (y-coordinate or ordinate) tells you how far up/down to go.

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: In which quadrant does the point (-3, 5) lie?
It lies in Quadrant II.

The x-coordinate is negative (-), meaning you move left. The y-coordinate is positive (+), meaning you move up. The combination of (-, +) always lands in the second quadrant!
Q2: If you plot the equation x - y = 0, which two quadrants will the line pass through?
Step 1: Rearrange the equation slightly: if x - y = 0, then y = x.
Step 2: If x is positive, y is positive (+, +). These points lie in Quadrant I.
Step 3: If x is negative, y is negative (-, -). These points lie in Quadrant III.
Final Answer: The line will pass perfectly diagonally through Quadrant I and Quadrant III!
