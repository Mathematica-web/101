The 360° Rule and Theorem 8.1

Just like every triangle is mathematically anchored to 180°, every single 4-sided figure is anchored to exactly 360°. Furthermore, when we draw a diagonal line across these shapes, incredible properties are revealed—especially in parallelograms!

Property / Theorem What it Means
Angle Sum Property The sum of all four interior angles of any quadrilateral is always exactly 360°.
Theorem 8.1 A diagonal of a parallelogram divides it into two congruent triangles.

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3:5:9:13. What is the measurement of the smallest angle?
Step 1: Let the angles be 3x, 5x, 9x, and 13x.
Step 2: By the Angle Sum Property, 3x + 5x + 9x + 13x = 360°.
Step 3: Combine terms: 30x = 360°, which means x = 12°.
Step 4: The smallest angle is 3x. So, 3 × 12° = 36°.
Final Answer: 36°
Q2: How do we prove Theorem 8.1 using congruence rules?
By using the ASA (Angle-Side-Angle) rule!

When a diagonal is drawn in a parallelogram, the alternate interior angles formed by the parallel opposite sides are equal. The diagonal itself is a common side shared by both triangles. Therefore, by Angle-Side-Angle, the two triangles must be mathematically congruent!
