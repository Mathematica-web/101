🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: How does the volume of a cylinder change if its radius is doubled but its height remains the same?
It becomes 4 times larger!

The formula for Volume is πr²h. Because the radius is squared, doubling it means (2r)² = 4r².
New Volume = π(2r)²h = 4(πr²h).
Q2: What is the ratio of the Curved Surface Area (CSA) to the Total Surface Area (TSA) of a cylinder where the radius equals the height (r = h)?
1:2

CSA = 2πrh. If r = h, then CSA = 2πr(r) = 2πr².
TSA = 2πr(r + h). If r = h, then TSA = 2πr(r + r) = 2πr(2r) = 4πr².
Ratio = 2πr² / 4πr² = 1/2.
