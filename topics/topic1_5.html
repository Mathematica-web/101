🚀 Laws of Exponents

Mastering the ultimate shortcut for repeated multiplication!

The Golden Rules for Real Numbers

Exponents (or powers) tell you how many times to multiply a base by itself. When combining expressions with the same base, follow these universal laws:

Rule Name Mathematical Formula Example
Multiplication Law am · an = am + n 23 · 22 = 25 = 32
Division Law am / an = am - n 54 / 52 = 52 = 25
Power of a Power (am)n = am · n (32)4 = 38
Power of a Product (a · b)m = am · bm (2 · 3)2 = 22 · 32 = 36
Negative Exponent a-n = 1 / an 4-2 = 1 / 42 = 1/16

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: What is the value of 1000?
The answer is 1.

The Zero Exponent Rule states that any non-zero base raised to the power of 0 is always equal to 1.
Proof pattern:
23 = 8
22 = 4 (Divided by 2)
21 = 2 (Divided by 2)
20 = 1 (Divided by 2)
Q2: Evaluate: (23)2 vs. 232. Are they the same?
No, they are different!

Case 1: (23)2
Apply the Power of a Power rule: multiply the exponents.
2(3 × 2) = 26 = 64.

Case 2: 232
Without parentheses, you evaluate the top exponents first. What is 32? It's 9.
So, the expression becomes 29 = 512.

Final Answer: 64 ≠ 512. Placement of parentheses matters!
