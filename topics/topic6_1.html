📏 Basic Terms and Definitions

The vocabulary you need to speak the language of Geometry!

The Foundation of Shapes

Before we can build complex geometric proofs, we need to agree on exactly what certain words mean. A single dot, a straight path, and the corner where they meet all have very specific mathematical definitions.

Geometric Term Definition
Line Segment A part of a line with two endpoints. It has a fixed length.
Ray A part of a line with one endpoint. It travels infinitely in one direction.
Collinear Points Three or more points that lie perfectly on the exact same line. Mathematically, they form a "triangle" with an Area of 0.
Angle Formed when two rays originate from the same endpoint. The rays are called arms, and the endpoint is the vertex.

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: If an angle measures exactly 180°, what type of angle is it?
A Straight Angle!

When an angle opens up exactly to 180°, the two arms of the angle point in perfectly opposite directions, forming a completely flat, straight line.
Q2: How many endpoints does a Line have compared to a Ray?
A Line has exactly 0 endpoints (it extends infinitely in both directions). A Ray has exactly 1 endpoint (it starts at a specific point and extends infinitely in the other direction).
