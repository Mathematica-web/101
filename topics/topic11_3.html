🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: If a cone and a cylinder have the exact same base radius and height, what is the ratio of their volumes?
1 : 3

The formula for the volume of a cylinder is πr²h. The formula for the volume of a cone is ⅓πr²h. Therefore, it takes exactly three identical cones to fill one matching cylinder!
Q2: A cone has a radius of 3cm and a height of 4cm. What is its slant height?
5 cm

Using the Pythagorean theorem inside the cone:
l = √(r² + h²)
l = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 cm.
