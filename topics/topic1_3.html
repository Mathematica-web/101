The Anatomy of a Rational Number

A rational number is any number that can be expressed as a simple fraction. Here are the core rules you must remember:

Concept Definition & Rule
The Core Formula Can be written as p / q, where p and q are integers.
Crucial Rule: q cannot be zero! (Division by zero is undefined).
Equivalent Fractions Different numbers can represent the exact same amount! Multiplying the top and bottom by the same number just cuts the pieces smaller, but doesn't change the size of the whole.
Density Property Between any two rational numbers, there is an infinite amount of other numbers. You can zoom in forever!

🧠 Test Your Knowledge

Q1: Are all Integers also considered Rational Numbers?
Yes, absolutely!

A rational number just needs to be written as p/q. You can turn any integer into a fraction simply by putting a 1 underneath it!
For example: -7 is the same as -7 / 1. It fits the rule perfectly!
Q2: If you cut a pizza into 3 slices and eat 1 slice, is that the exact same amount of pizza as cutting it into 6 slices and eating 2?
Yes! That is an Equivalent Fraction.

If you take 1/3 and multiply both the top and bottom by 2, you get 2/6. The math proves that the physical amount of food you ate remains exactly the same; only the size of the individual slices changed.
